## Episode Structure

Each episode should follow this shape:

1. **Hook (0-3s):** Impossible or emotionally charged opening line.
2. **Context (3-20s):** Who, where, and why we care.
3. **Escalation (20-45s):** A choice, deadline, or reveal.
4. **Cliffhanger (45-90s):** A specific question that makes viewers want the next part.
