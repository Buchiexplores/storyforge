# Episode 9: Tomorrow Calls Back

At midnight, Tobi finally answered the call in the stairwell.

The voice on the other end was his own, older and ragged, as if every word had been running for miles.

Future Tobi begged him to stop saving people the phone had chosen.

He said every rescue moved the debt closer to Mara.

The cracked screen flashed over the call.

New message: He is lying to keep you obedient.

Future Tobi shouted through the interference and told him not to trust anything the screen said.

Delete your number before sunrise, he said, or tomorrow will always find you.

Mara heard enough from the doorway to ask the one question Tobi could not bear.

Am I already dead somewhere?

Before he could answer, the phone sent an address to an abandoned telecom exchange by the lagoon.

Tobi, Mara, and Iya Sade reached it while night rain rolled in over the empty yard.

Inside, dead desk phones rang on their own in long rows.

Every receiver carried a different version of Tobi's voice asking for help.

Future Tobi said the final save would create a replacement first owner.

The replacement was Mara.

Then the bus woman stepped out from between the ringing switchboards and said the deleted futures were using Tobi as a doorway back into the world.

The screen burned with one last choice.

Delete my number and lose her forever. Keep it and save her once.

By the time dawn touched the windows, Tobi was still holding the phone.
