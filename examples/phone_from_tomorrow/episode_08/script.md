# Episode 8: Battery At One Percent

By sunrise, the phone still showed one percent.

Every wall socket, power bank, and charging cable Tobi tried went dead the moment it touched the cracked screen.

New message: Battery rises only for what you refuse to say.

Mara made him test it on the street by telling a bike mechanic the truth about why his hands would not stop shaking.

The battery climbed, then dropped again when Tobi lied to his dispatch boss about missing deliveries.

Iya Sade said the phone eats borrowed futures, but it drinks spoken truth.

So Tobi and Mara spent the day feeding it confessions across Lagos.

He admitted to Mara that part of him liked being the only one who could stop disaster.

She admitted that some mornings she remembered the phone before she remembered her own dreams.

At a traffic jam under hard noon light, the bus woman appeared beside Tobi's bike and said the battery was preparing for one call only.

The phone reached nine percent when Tobi told the truth about their mother.

He said if tomorrow offered him one more day with her, he would betray almost anything to take it.

The battery flashed full green, then fell back to one percent.

New message: Charge complete. Voice waiting at midnight.

That night, Tobi unplugged every device in the apartment and made Mara promise not to answer any ringing phone.

At 11:59 PM, the city light outside their window blinked out block by block.

The cracked phone began to ring from Tobi's own number.

He carried it to the stairwell alone while Mara watched from the doorway, terrified.

Before he could answer, a second message burned across the screen.

If she hears this call, she hears her funeral.
