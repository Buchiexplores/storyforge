# Episode 5: Mara's Name

Tobi got home before sunset and found Mara staring at the cracked phone like it already belonged to her.

She said it had rung once in his room even though he had carried it all day.

New message: Mara Adeyemi. Say nothing until she says your name.

For one full minute, Mara looked at him like a stranger and asked who he was.

Then memory rushed back and she dropped the bowl in her hands.

Tobi finally told her about the messages, the bus, and Iya Sade's warning about debt.

Mara did not panic. She opened her notebook and showed him a street she had drawn in her sleep three nights in a row.

Ojo Crescent was on the page, but not on any map in Lagos.

The phone vibrated again.

If she answers the second call, the street keeps her.

At 11:14 PM, Mara's old keypad phone rang from Tobi's own number while the cracked phone stayed warm in his pocket.

He let the first call die. The apartment lights blinked once.

Thirty seconds later, the second call came from a number saved as Mum, even though their mother's SIM had been buried years ago.

Mara reached for it before Tobi could stop her.

The cracked phone screamed green.

Break the window.

Tobi smashed the rain-stuck window beside the front door just as the hallway filled with the sound of a ringing phone from outside.

On the landing sat the breathing package, and tucked beneath its tape was a note in Mara's handwriting.

The second call stopped. The package stopped breathing. Mara whispered that she had written the same words in her sleep.

The note said: Come before the street disappears.
