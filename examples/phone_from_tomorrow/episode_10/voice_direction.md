# Voice Direction

Keep the same narrator voice as the earlier episodes. This is the finale, so let it carry the weight of everything before it.

Performance:
- open hushed and exhausted, like someone who has not slept in days
- let Mara's refusal land clear and unafraid
- give Iya Sade's revelation the gravity of a long-kept secret finally spoken
- build steadily through the ringing phones to the moment Tobi dials his own number
- play the delete as quiet resolve, not triumph
- soften into relief and grief at the dawn, then close gently
- end the final line warm and direct to the audience, no cliffhanger pressure
