# Storyforge Channel Bible

## Chosen Lane

Afro-futurist mystery thriller.

## Why This Lane

This lane gives the channel a strong identity instead of competing with generic horror, Reddit stories, or recycled creepypasta. It can combine:
- modern African cities
- technology that feels almost real
- spiritual tension without needing explicit gore
- moral choices
- serial cliffhangers

It is also production-friendly. Most scenes can be told through narration, phone screens, city ambience, close-ups, silhouettes, and short AI-generated cinematic shots.

## Channel Concept

Working channel name:

`Tomorrow Came Early`

Alternate names:
- The Tomorrow Files
- Midnight Signal
- Strange Lagos
- Future Messages
- Dispatches From Tomorrow

Recommended:

`Tomorrow Came Early`

Reason: it is short, memorable, and broad enough for many story arcs.

## Core Promise

Every episode tells a high-stakes fictional story where an ordinary person receives impossible information before it happens.

The viewer should feel:
- "I need to know what happens next."
- "This could become a series."
- "The world feels specific, not generic."

## Series 1

Title:

`The Phone That Receives Tomorrow`

Premise:

A Lagos delivery rider finds an old phone that receives text messages exactly 24 hours before disasters happen. At first, the messages help him save strangers. Then the phone starts asking for impossible choices.

## Main Character

Name: Tobi Adeyemi

Age: 24

Life:
- delivery rider in Lagos
- supports his younger sister
- practical, tired, funny when nervous
- not a superhero

Need:

He wants one lucky break.

Flaw:

He thinks every problem can be solved if he moves fast enough.

## Tone

Modern, tense, intimate, cinematic.

Voice:
- direct
- sensory
- emotional
- no purple prose
- no fake old-timey myth language

Reference feel:
- urban mystery
- near-future thriller
- moral fable
- slow-burn supernatural technology

## Platform Strategy

### TikTok

Primary format:
- 60 to 90 seconds per episode
- vertical 9:16
- hard hook in first 2 seconds
- one dramatic turn by 30 seconds
- cliffhanger ending

TikTok Creator Rewards note:
- target videos longer than one minute
- keep them original and high-quality
- avoid Duets, Stitches, reused footage, and generic templates

### YouTube Shorts

Use the same 60 to 90 second story, tightened if needed.

YouTube note:
- keep each episode visually and narratively distinct
- avoid image slideshows with minimal story variation
- use original scripts, original narration, generated or licensed visuals, and visible creative direction

### YouTube Long-Form

Weekly compilation:
- Episodes 1 to 5 stitched together
- 8 to 12 minutes
- add a short "previously" intro
- add ambient transitions
- title as a mini-movie

Example:

`The Phone That Receives Tomorrow | Episodes 1-5`

## Originality Rules

Every episode must have:
- a unique moral choice
- at least one specific local detail
- a new visual motif
- a clear consequence from the previous episode
- a human decision, not only "the phone told him"

Avoid:
- celebrity likenesses
- real breaking news disasters
- copyrighted characters
- reused movie clips
- AI-generated public figure voices
- repeating the same structure with different names only

## Episode Formula

1. Impossible message.
2. Normal person reacts.
3. Specific deadline.
4. Choice that costs something.
5. Consequence.
6. New message or reveal.

## Visual Language

Recurring motifs:
- cracked phone screen
- yellow streetlights after rain
- traffic reflections
- unread messages
- danfo buses in motion
- generator hum
- close-up hands
- city seen through wet glass

Color:
- black
- amber
- green phone glow
- wet asphalt blue
- red brake lights

## Safety and Platform Notes

- If using realistic AI-generated visuals, disclose when required by the platform.
- Keep violence implied, not graphic.
- Do not depict real public figures or real disasters.
- Make it clear in descriptions that this is a fictional story.
- Save prompts, scripts, and edit timelines so you can prove creative process if monetization is reviewed.
