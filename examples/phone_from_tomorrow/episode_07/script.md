# Episode 7: The First Owner

Tobi dropped the warm dead batteries and the receipt with tomorrow's date onto Iya Sade's stall before dawn.

For the first time, the old woman looked afraid of the cracked phone.

She said the phone had belonged to someone in her family before Tobi ever touched it.

The first owner lasted seven days and spent every warning trying to save people who never knew his name.

Iya Sade opened her palm and showed Tobi a matching dead battery she had kept for years.

The cracked phone lit green between them.

New message: The first owner did not vanish. She hid him.

Mara stared at Iya Sade until the woman finally led them behind a locked cinema near the market.

Inside a dust-choked projection room, names and dates covered the walls in layers of chalk.

Every name ended on day seven.

On a wooden table sat a strange charger made from stripped copper wire, prayer beads, and old phone parts.

When Tobi plugged the phone into it, the screen stayed at one percent.

Then the message changed.

Power does not take current. It takes confession.

Tobi admitted he had started to need the warnings because they made him feel chosen.

The battery climbed to two percent.

Mara confessed she had wanted to hear the second call because she missed their mother's voice.

The battery climbed again, and Iya Sade finally told the truth: the first owner was not her brother. It was her son, Seyi.

In the cracked projector glass, the bus woman appeared and said seven days ends when the phone learns your truest lie.

New message: Keep the battery alive until midnight.
