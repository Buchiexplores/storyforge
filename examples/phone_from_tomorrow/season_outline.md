# Season 1 Outline

Series title:

`The Phone That Receives Tomorrow`

## Logline

When a Lagos delivery rider finds a cracked phone that receives messages from tomorrow, he starts saving strangers from small disasters until the messages begin trading lives for answers.

## Season Engine

Every message gives Tobi a chance to prevent something. Every prevention changes tomorrow. The phone is not predicting the future. It is negotiating with it.

## Core Mystery

Who is sending the messages?

Season answer:

The messages are not from one person. They are from the lives Tobi saves, speaking from futures that no longer exist.

## Main Cast

Tobi Adeyemi:
- 24-year-old delivery rider
- stubborn, fast, protective
- thinks movement solves pain

Mara Adeyemi:
- Tobi's younger sister
- sharp, observant, studying for exams
- becomes the first person to suspect the phone is changing Tobi

Iya Sade:
- street food seller near the bus stop
- knows more old stories than she admits
- says the city has always had ways of warning people

The Unknown Number:
- sender of the messages
- starts helpful
- becomes personal

## Episode Arc

### Episode 1: Do Not Open The Door

Tobi receives a message from his own number warning him not to open his door at 7:40 the next morning. He ignores it until the warning proves specific.

### Episode 2: The Bus With No Driver

The phone sends a bus plate number and a time. Tobi must decide whether to ruin his delivery job to chase a warning no one believes.

### Episode 3: Save Her Twice

Tobi saves a woman from an accident, then receives a second message saying she was supposed to die tomorrow instead.

### Episode 4: The Price Of A Minute

Every time Tobi changes the future, someone near him loses one minute of memory.

### Episode 5: Mara's Name

The phone sends Mara's name with no instructions.

### Episode 6: The Street That Disappears

Tobi follows directions to a street no map remembers.

### Episode 7: The First Owner

Iya Sade reveals that the phone has had owners before, and none kept it for more than seven days.

### Episode 8: Battery At One Percent

The phone cannot be charged by electricity. It charges when Tobi tells the truth.

### Episode 9: Tomorrow Calls Back

Tobi finally answers a call from tomorrow and hears his own voice begging him to stop.

### Episode 10: Delete My Number

The season ends with Tobi choosing whether to destroy the phone or use it to save Mara.

## Season Rule

The phone never says everything. It only gives enough information to force a choice.

## Cliffhanger Style

End each episode with:
- a new impossible message
- a revealed cost
- a name the audience recognizes
- a contradiction in what Tobi thought was true

Avoid fake cliffhangers that reset immediately.
