## Narration Script

At 2:13 in the morning, Tobi's phone buzzed under his pillow.

The message came from his own number.

`Do not open your door tomorrow at 7:40 AM.`

Tobi stared at it for a long time, then laughed because fear is easier to swallow when you pretend it is stupid.

His real phone had been stolen three days ago. This one was a cracked old thing he found inside a delivery bag no customer ever claimed.

By morning, Lagos was already awake. Generators coughing. Buses shouting. Rainwater shining under streetlights that had forgotten to turn off.

At 7:39, Tobi stood beside his door with one shoe on.

His younger sister Mara called from the kitchen, "Are you leaving or praying?"

Then someone knocked.

Three soft taps.

Tobi checked the time.

7:40.

The phone buzzed again.

`If you open it, she will know where you live.`

Outside, a woman's voice said, "Delivery for Tobi Adeyemi."

But Tobi had not ordered anything.

And no one in that building knew his full name.
