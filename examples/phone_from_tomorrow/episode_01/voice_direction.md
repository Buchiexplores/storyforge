# Voice Direction

## Voice

Calm, intimate, suspenseful narrator.

The voice should feel like someone telling you a secret at night, not like a movie trailer.

## Pace

Medium-slow.

Pause after:
- "The message came from his own number."
- "7:40."
- "But Tobi had not ordered anything."

## Emotion

Start quiet and strange.

Build tension after the first warning.

Stay restrained at the ending. Do not over-act the package reveal.

## Pronunciation

Tobi: TOH-bee

Mara: MAH-rah

Lagos: LAY-goss or LAH-goss, choose one and stay consistent.

Adeyemi: Ah-day-YEH-mee

## ElevenLabs Direction

```text
Narration style: intimate suspense storytelling, calm but tense, modern mystery tone, natural pauses, no trailer voice, no shouting, emotionally controlled, cinematic documentary pacing.
```

## Human Recording Direction

Record in a quiet room.

Keep mouth 6 to 8 inches from microphone.

Read slower than normal conversation.

Do two takes:
- one restrained
- one more tense

Use the restrained take for the beginning and the tense take for the door scene if editing manually.
