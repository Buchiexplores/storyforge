Vertical 9:16.

Target duration:

70 to 85 seconds.

Resolution:

1080x1920 or higher.

## Sound Design

Use:
- phone buzz
- distant generator hum
- rain ambience
- muted traffic
- soft three-knock SFX
- low cinematic pulse

Avoid:
- loud jump scares
- copyrighted music
- recognizable songs

## Caption Style

Use clean bold captions:
- white text
- subtle black shadow
- 2 lines max
- keep captions away from bottom UI area

Highlight key phrases in green:
- "own number"
- "7:40"
- "she will know"

## Pacing Rule

Change visual every 3 to 5 seconds unless the moment needs stillness.

The door knock moment should feel slower than the rest.
