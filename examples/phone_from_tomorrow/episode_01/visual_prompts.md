## Motion Instructions

If generating video clips:
- Keep each clip 4 to 6 seconds.
- Use slow push-in, slight handheld movement, or static tension.
- Avoid fast action.
- Avoid showing distorted hands close-up unless needed.
- Do not use real logos or real personal data.
